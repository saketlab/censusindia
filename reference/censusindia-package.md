# censusindia: Census of India data for R

Digitised Census of India data from 1901 to 2011: population time
series, primary census abstracts at state and district levels, mother
tongue tables, SC/ST and tribal populations, and administrative
directories. Census variables can be retrieved with geographic
boundaries attached for mapping.
